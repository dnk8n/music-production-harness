# Love Train remixes

**Status:** draft (remix 1 started 2026-11-14)

Remixes of "Love Train" by PillowTalk x Soul Clap, featuring Greg Paulus and
Crew Love, for Dean's own listening and never to be released (2026-11-13).
Every render carries `licence:private` (REQ-MST-04). Dean's guide to the
styles, in order: Detroit techno with Jersey garage, Afro with acid techno,
jungle with djembe, clean Detroit, dub techno and acid tech. Each remix is
about 8 minutes, one Renoise song with short blocks rated first (REQ-TRK-03),
and each is finished before the next starts. Research:
[Remix practice for Love Train](https://claude.ai/artifact/Ru96Afswp6zig5Lgd8M1zv)
(2026-11-14).

The source is Dean's copy in his full tracks folder (FLAC, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit,
5:37.5). It sits on a fixed grid of exactly 120 BPM, and its bar 1 starts at
its first sample, so bar *n* starts at (*n* - 1) x 2 s (REQ-CAT-10). Its
passages, from a bar-by-bar map of the full mix (levels in four bands, stereo

## Love Train remix 1: Detroit x Jersey

Dean's first pick (2026-11-13): Detroit techno meets Jersey garage, at the
source's own 120 BPM, so the vocals are never stretched. The plan has 240
bars (8:00). The source's train passage drops the groove twice, at bars 29-32
and 141-144, as it does in the original. Under it go BM-DRUM-02's Jersey
shuffle with Detroit grit, an M1-style organ playing the source's bassline,
and Detroit minor stabs.

The first version is a re-edit, as the research advises. It places the
source's own bars in the plan's form, and plays BM-DRUM-02's Jersey shuffle,
with the sounds of its fresh take, wherever no source bars play. Later
versions replace source bars, section by section, with new parts: the organ
bass, stabs, strings, sends, ducking and the vocal from the stems.

The kit is BM-DRUM-02's pattern at 120 BPM. The closed hats choke the open
hat, and only the off-beat sixteenths swing, so kick and clap stay straight.
The source plays at its own level: about -12 LUFS in its grooves, so the kit
sections, at the kit's matched levels, sit a few dB under it.

Hear: a DJ-friendly 8 minutes. Sixteen bars of swung kick and hats build to
the clap. Then everything stops for the train, the original's groove drops
on bar 33, and the song runs in the plan's order with its sections stretched
and repeated. The train returns at bar 141 to drop the chorus again, and the
source's own last train comes before the kit carries the outro. Every cut
lands on a bar line, with no clicks, no gaps and no change of speed or pitch.
- **Why:** the research's cheapest end-to-end test: an 8-minute re-edit built
  from source bars tests bar cutting, long samples, rendering and delivery
  before stems or new sounds exist. Its form is the remix's, so each later
  version only changes what plays in a section.
- **Verify:** measure, listen

### BM-LTDJ-01: Remix 1, bars 25-48: the build, the train and the groove
120 BPM, 16th swing 58%. Bars 25-48 of BM-LTDJ-05.

Hear: four bars of the full Jersey kit, then everything stops and the train
plays alone, its horn swelling and bending. The original groove drops on bar
9 of the block, exactly as the original's dropped at 0:24.
- **Why:** the research's first question about remix 1: does the departure
  land the groove? It joins a new kit to the source and cues the first clip.
- **Verify:** measure, listen

### BM-LTDJ-02: Remix 1, bars 65-96: verse and chorus
120 BPM, 16th swing 58%. Bars 65-96 of BM-LTDJ-05.

Hear: the original's first verse and chorus, untouched in this version. Later
versions put the organ bass and stabs under the vocal here.
- **Why:** the research's second question: does the vocal sit on the new bass
  and chords? This block is where that will be heard first.
- **Verify:** measure, listen

### BM-LTDJ-03: Remix 1, bars 125-152: breakdown, the train again and the peak
120 BPM, 16th swing 58%. Bars 125-152 of BM-LTDJ-05.

Hear: the last four bars of the second verse, cued from the bar the track has
reached, then twelve bars of the third verse. The train plays alone again and
drops the chorus at bar 21 of the block.
- **Why:** the research's third question: does the breakdown hold, and does
  the second departure drop the peak? Its first clip starts before the block
  does, so it tests an excerpt cueing a clip from the middle.
- **Verify:** measure, listen

### BM-LTDJ-04: Remix 1, bars 177-208: dub-out and the last train
120 BPM, 16th swing 58%. Bars 177-208 of BM-LTDJ-05.

Hear: the source's verse-like passage and the start of its outro, then eight
bars of the full kit. The source's own second train ends the block, where the
DJ outro takes over in the whole track.
- **Why:** the research's fourth question: does the dub-out hold without the
  full vocal? This version tests the kit coming back between source
  passages.
- **Verify:** measure, listen
